# The agent job brief

Before I build any agent, I write this page. If I can't fill it in, the agent isn't ready to exist.

People move with ambition. Agents don't have any. They need a clear beginning, middle and end, and this brief gives them that.

---

**1. Job title**
One job, one owner. "Ads search-term cleaner," not "marketing helper."

**2. Beginning: what starts it**
A schedule, a new message, a new lead. Be specific.

**3. Middle: what it does**
Three to five steps, in order. If you need more than five, it's two agents.

**4. End: what "done" looks like**
The exact output and where it goes. A Slack post in one channel, a draft for approval, a row in a sheet.

**5. It can do alone**
List it.

**6. It must ask first**
Money, anything sent to another person, anything deleted.

**7. It must never**
Invent facts, report what it didn't check, post outside its channel.

**8. How I'll know it worked**
One number you'll look at in a week.
