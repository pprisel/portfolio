# PM-001 AI blog posts that almost erased us from Google

**Severity:** High · **Area:** Organic search

## What happened

Before I joined, the plan for organic growth was volume: lots of AI-written blog posts, produced without review. The same content was copied and pasted across many posts. Dozens of pages started competing for the same keywords.

## Impact

Keyword cannibalization. Google couldn't tell which page to rank, so none of them ranked well. For a ten-year-old business that lives on search, left alone, this could have made it disappear from Google.

## Root cause

AI was used to produce volume with no owner, no review and no keyword map. The tool wasn't the problem. The missing process was.

## What changed

- A daily SEO agent now maps every keyword to one page and flags overlaps
- Competing pages get consolidated, redirected or rewritten
- No content goes live without a keyword it owns

## Result

Top-10 keywords on one brand went from 23 to 65 in eight weeks. #1 keywords on another went from 38 to 84. See the [SEO/GEO Operator case study](../case-studies/02-seo-geo-operator.md).
