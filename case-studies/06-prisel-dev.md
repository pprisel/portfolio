# prisel.dev

**My personal site, built entirely with prompts.**

🌐 Live: [prisel.dev](https://prisel.dev)

> The site is live. The source is not published here.

## The brief I gave myself

- English and Portuguese, with an obvious language switch
- Clean, high-tech look: white and gray, with Claude orange
- Easy for humans to scan and easy for LLMs and crawlers to read
- Lists every course, talk and service in one place

## How I built it

I described what I wanted, iterated with Claude Code, and deployed on Vercel. No templates, no hand-written code.

## Built for AI readers too

- An [`llms.txt`](https://prisel.dev/llms.txt) file so AI assistants can read who I am and what I offer
- Structured, semantic content instead of text hidden in images

## Stack

Claude Code · Vercel · Google Workspace (email on the domain)
