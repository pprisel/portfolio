# Brand Kit for LLMs

**Brand guidelines written for agents to read, so everything they make stays on brand.**

> Structure and approach only. The brand files themselves are private.

## The problem

Brand guidelines are usually PDFs made for designers. Agents, Claude and website builders can't use them well. The result is generic copy and off-brand colors.

## What I built

Two versions of the guidelines for each brand:

- **Human version:** visual, with examples, for the team
- **LLM version:** plain structured text with exact color codes, font names, logo rules, tone of voice, words to use and words to avoid

Every agent reads the LLM version before it writes copy, designs a piece or edits a page.

## What goes in the LLM version

Colors, type, logo rules, voice, and the client names and numbers the brand is allowed to use. The exact structure, and the banned-phrase list that does most of the work, are taught in the course.

## Why it matters

The single biggest giveaway of AI content is that it sounds like AI. A strict voice section with banned phrases does more for quality than a better model.
