# ADR-003 Drafts, never deliveries

**Status:** Adopted, October 2026

## Context

Colleagues in sales and operations ask marketing for things all day: a design piece, a deck, a reply. Agents can produce these fast. If they send them directly, nobody checks quality and nobody knows what marketing promised.

## Decision

When a colleague asks for something, the agent does the work and posts it for my approval. Nothing reaches the person who asked without my yes.

## Consequences

- Requests get done the same day, but I stay accountable for what leaves marketing
- The team sees one consistent voice, not 30 agents with opinions
- Approving is one tap on my phone
