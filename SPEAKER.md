## Talks

**The Night Shift: how I run a marketing department with 30 AI agents**
What a real agent team looks like day to day: what each agent owns, what it may never do, and what broke along the way. For marketing leaders, founders and AI teams.

How a marketer with no coding background learned to build agents, websites and automations with Claude. For any audience that thinks tech is someone else's job.
